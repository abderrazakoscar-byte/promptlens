FAQ

What does the tool do?

It looks at your image and writes a text prompt that describes the subject, style, colors, lighting and composition, so you can recreate or remix it with an AI image generator.

Which formats are supported?

PNG, JPG, WEBP and GIF. Large images are shrunk in your browser before they are sent.

Are my images stored?

Images are sent only to generate the prompt. This app does not save them on a server.

Is adult content allowed?

No. Adult images are rejected.
